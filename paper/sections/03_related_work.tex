\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Physics-based motion tracking.} PHC, PULSE, ExBody2, GMT, and other H2O-style trackers
learn high-fidelity imitation policies under physical simulation, but all assume a single,
fixed-morphology character; none address generalization across a body-shape distribution.

\paragraph{Morphology-conditioned RL.} HUMOS \todo{cite} conditions motion generation (via
shape-conditioned diffusion) on SMPL body shape, producing per-shape-adapted reference motions
rather than a naively retargeted, shape-invariant one. MorFiC \todo{cite} \todo{one-line summary}.
Our work builds directly on HUMOS's motion data to supply a shape-\emph{dependent} training signal
(Section~\ref{sec:method}), but targets a control policy rather than a motion generator.

\paragraph{Mixture-of-experts in actor-critic RL.} \todo{cite prior MoE-in-RL work}. We evaluate
MoE as an architecture candidate for handling motion diversity (Section~\ref{sec:experiments}) and
find it does not separate from a capacity-matched dense baseline in our setting.
