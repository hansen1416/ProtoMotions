We present a single physics-based control policy that imitates arbitrary human motions across
128 distinct SMPL body shapes (26--144\,kg, 1.13--1.67\,m), in contrast to prior physics-based
motion trackers that are restricted to a single fixed morphology. Our approach combines a
two-stage curriculum (neutral pretraining followed by shape transfer) that decouples motion
out-of-distribution generalization from shape out-of-distribution generalization, trained at full
HumanML3D scale (20{,}946 clips). We introduce a shape-invariant, DOF-angle-space evaluation
protocol that isolates skill consistency from body-scale confounds, and show that a single policy
achieves consistent joint-articulation tracking across the full shape distribution.
\end{abstract}
