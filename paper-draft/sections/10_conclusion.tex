\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We construct a paired multi-morphology corpus and train one physics-based
controller while preserving correspondence between source clips, generated
references, and simulation assets. The system integrates shape-conditioned
generation, reference refinement, temporal slot/type attention, and streamed
training across 128 body configurations.

The original checkpoint achieves 99.14\% success on 1,048 independent test
clips with one selected trained body each. A separate 32-body replay achieves
91.64\% overall success, with strong transfer on 28 bodies and severe errors
on four unresolved cases. This establishes substantial shared tracking and
transfer within the sampled range while exposing non-uniform robustness.
Repeated-seed comparisons and paired reference audits clarify the design
trade-offs. Fixed all-body held-out coverage, causal conditioning controls,
and resolution of body-specific failures are the next steps toward stronger
generalization claims.
